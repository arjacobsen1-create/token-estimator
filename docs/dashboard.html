Scenario: baseline vs optimised routing

The business decision this dashboard supports. Baseline runs everything on the frontier model. Optimised keeps quality-critical work (drafting, code) on the frontier and routes high-volume reading work (summarise, classify, extract) to the fast, cheap tier.
